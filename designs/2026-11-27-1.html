PET CARE TIP

New toy in the cart? Check it first.

Deal days mean new toys. A quick check before playtime:

Bigger than their mouthPick a size that can't be swallowed whole.
Tags and ribbons offRemove strings, labels and packaging before play.
Watch the first sessionsSee how they chew it, and bin it once it breaks apart.
PETTO tip: add a note to your pet's profile about which toys they love, and which ones they destroy.
