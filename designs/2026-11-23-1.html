MONDAY TIP

Brushing your dog's teeth? Start small.

1Pet toothpaste only. Human toothpaste can contain xylitol, which is toxic to dogs.
2Begin with a finger and a few front teeth, then work up to a soft pet brush.
3Keep it short and happy. Praise, stop early, try again tomorrow.
4Red gums or bad breath? Ask your vet about a dental check.

Add "brush teeth" to the routine.
