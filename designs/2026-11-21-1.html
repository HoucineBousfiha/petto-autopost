WINTER TIP

Antifreeze tastes sweet. It's toxic to pets.

Wipe up drips and spills in the garage or driveway straight away.
Store it sealed and up high, where paws and tongues can't reach.
Think they licked some? Call your vet right away, don't wait for signs.
PETTO keeps vet visits and health notes together, so you're ready to call.
