COLD-WEATHER TIP

Back from a winter walk? Check the paws.

Wipe paws with a damp cloth. Road salt and de-icers can irritate pads, and pets lick them off.
Look between the toes for ice balls, cuts or cracked pads.
Limping or licking a lot? Ask your vet.
