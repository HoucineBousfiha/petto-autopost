MORNING TIP

Their teeth need brushing too.

Small, regular brushing helps keep plaque down. Go slow and keep it calm.

1Use pet toothpaste only. Human toothpaste isn't safe for pets.
2Start with a finger brush and a few seconds, then build up.
3Bad breath, red gums or chewing on one side? Ask your vet.
