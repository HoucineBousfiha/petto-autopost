MORNING TIP

Keep these snacks off the coffee table.

ChocolateEspecially dark and baking chocolate
Grapes & raisinsEven a small handful can be risky for dogs
Onions & garlicRaw, cooked or powdered, for cats and dogs
Sugar-free gumOften contains xylitol
Snack went missing? Call your vet right away. PETTO keeps their number one tap away.
