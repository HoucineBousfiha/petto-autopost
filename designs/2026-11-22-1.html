WINTER TIP

Back from a winter walk? Check the paws.

Wipe paws with a damp cloth to remove road salt and de-icer before they lick it off.
Look between the toes for ice clumps, small cuts or redness.
Cracked or sore pads? Ask your vet about paw balm or booties.
Add a "paw check" to the evening walk in PETTO, so nobody forgets.
