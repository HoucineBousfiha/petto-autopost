# PETTO asset library (`brand/assets/`)

Use in designs as `../brand/assets/<file>`. Rotation rules are in `BRAND.md` → "Assets".
Suits: **tip** / **fun** / **feature** / **engagement**. Background: what the asset sits well on
(**light** = cream, **dark** = navy, **both**). "Faces/text" = keep it out of the 100px side margins.
